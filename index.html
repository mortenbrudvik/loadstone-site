Privacy Policy

Loadstone for Windows · Effective 9 September 2026 · Publisher: Morten Brudvik

Loadstone is a free, tray-only window-tiling utility for Windows. It snaps the focused window into halves, corners, thirds, and two-thirds using keyboard shortcuts or optional drag-to-edge snapping.

What we collect

Loadstone collects and transmits nothing.

  • No personal information
  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers, or crash reports
  • No accounts, sign-in, or online services
  • No network requests of any kind

Data stored on your device

Preferences you set in the app (for example keyboard shortcuts and whether drag-to-edge snapping is enabled) and optional local log files are stored only on your computer, typically under your user profile’s local app data folder. That data never leaves your device.

Children

Loadstone is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
