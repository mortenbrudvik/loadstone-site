Loadstone for Windows

A tray utility that snaps windows into halves, corners and thirds.

HelpPrivacy policy

Loadstone sits in the notification area and stays out of the way. When you need two documents side by side, a browser in a third, or a window parked in a corner, it moves the focused window to a precise tile of the display's work area, leaving the taskbar uncovered.

It is the Windows port of the macOS Loadstone, with the same commands and the same defaults.

Get Loadstone from the Microsoft Store. It is free. On Windows you can also open it in the Microsoft Store app instead of downloading the Store's installer.
